# Response to Referees — Template

Replace the bracketed material. Keep the structure: quote, respond, state the change, quote the new
text. Every comment gets an answer, including the ones you disagree with.

---

**Manuscript:** [MS number] — "[Title]"

[N] substantive points; addressing them has [improved X / clarified Y / led us to add Z]. The most
significant changes are [one sentence], [one sentence], and [one sentence]. All changes are marked
in blue in the revised manuscript.

Our point-by-point responses follow. Referee comments are in italics; quoted revised text is indented.

## Reminders while writing this letter

- Answer **every** point, including ones you consider trivial. A skipped comment costs a round.
- Change the manuscript wherever possible. "We have clarified this in the text" is only credible with
  the new text quoted.
- If a referee misunderstood, the manuscript was unclear — fix the manuscript, not just the referee.
- Concede clearly and early when the referee is right; it builds credibility for the points where you
  push back.
- Keep the tone even. The editor reads the whole exchange.
- Never fabricate a measurement, a number, or a reference to escape a request.
